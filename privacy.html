Privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026.

The short version. Your pod is yours. We store it so you can use it, we never sell it, we never show ads, and we never read it to profile you. Apps only see what you allow, and you can take everything with you or delete it at any time.

Who we are

skpods.id and the SKPods pod service at pods.skpods.id are run by the smilinTux project. Questions about privacy: privacy@skpods.id.

What we keep, and why

  • Your account: your user name, your email address, a password hash (never the password itself), optional two-factor or passkey settings, and your quota. We need these to run your account and to help you get back in.
  • Your public profile: only the fields you switch to Public are shown at your WebID. Everything else stays private.
  • Your pod: the files and data you or your apps save. We store them for you and serve them only to you and to the people and apps you grant access to. Uploads are scanned for viruses automatically.
  • Access grants: which apps and people you allowed into which parts of your pod, so the pod can enforce them.
  • Technical logs: IP address, time, the address requested and the result, kept for up to 30 days to keep the service secure and working, then deleted.

We use one sign-in cookie to keep you logged in. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising cookies.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade your data.
  • Show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • Read your pod to profile you, or use it to train AI models.
  • Give an app more access than you granted.

Where your data lives

The pod server runs at Oracle Cloud in Chicago, United States, behind Cloudflare, which carries the network traffic. Pod files may be stored with Cloudflare R2. If you choose to keep your pod files with another storage provider, such as Google Drive or OneDrive, your files are stored there under your own account with that provider, and that provider's privacy policy also applies.

Connected storage (Google Drive, OneDrive and others)

If you connect a storage provider, we ask for the narrowest access it offers: on Google Drive, only files skpods.id creates; on OneDrive, only the Apps/skpods folder. We use that access only to store and read your own pod files on your behalf. Sign-in tokens are encrypted, never logged, and never shared with apps. You can disconnect at any time, which revokes our access at the provider.

skpods.id's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not let people read it except with your permission or where the law requires it, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Who else touches your data

Only the services that run the platform: Oracle (hosting), Cloudflare (network and storage), and, when you choose it, your own storage provider. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it, and we will tell you unless the law forbids it.

Your choices

  • See and change: everything in your pod and profile, from your account.
  • Take it with you: download your pod, or move it to another Solid server or storage provider.
  • Delete: deleting your account deletes your pod and profile from our server. Backups roll off within 30 days.
  • Ask us: write to privacy@skpods.id for a copy of what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it.

Children

skpods.id is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so on this page and email account holders before it takes effect.
